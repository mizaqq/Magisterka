# Question 45: What kind of models can be used in the analysis of the potential and the range of market?

## Answer

Market-potential and range analyses rely on spatial interaction and demand estimation models. The gravity model relates sales at a location to attraction factors—store size, assortment, marketing spend—and inversely to travel distance, helping retailers choose catchment areas. The Huff probabilistic model refines this by converting attraction and distance into choice probabilities, yielding market share surfaces for competing outlets. For broader geographic coverage, regression-based demand models link consumption to socioeconomic covariates such as income density and purchasing power, while panel data variants capture temporal dynamics. Logistic and Poisson geospatial regressions estimate penetration or visit counts across postcode grids. When detailed mobility data are available, agent-based simulations model individual shopping trips to evaluate cannibalisation and saturation. Finally, machine-learning approaches—gradient boosting on point-of-sale data or spatial random forests—predict untapped demand hotspots. Combining these models with GIS visualisation produces evidence-based site selection and expansion strategies.
