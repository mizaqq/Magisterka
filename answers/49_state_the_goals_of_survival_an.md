# Question 49: State the goals of survival analysis.

**Status:** ✅ Completed | **Words:** 163 | **Generated:** 2025-07-04T00:33:00Z

---

## Answer

Survival analysis seeks to understand and predict the distribution of time until an event—failure, churn, death—occurs. Its primary goals are: 1) estimate survival and hazard functions to summarise baseline risk over time; 2) compare survival experiences of different groups via log-rank or Fleming–Harrington tests; 3) quantify covariate effects on event timing using models like Cox proportional hazards or parametric Weibull regressions, yielding interpretable hazard ratios; 4) handle right-, left- or interval-censored observations without bias; 5) produce individual or cohort-level risk predictions for decision support, such as maintenance scheduling or patient prognosis; and 6) evaluate model fit and assumptions, including proportional hazards, through residuals and time-dependent tests. Collectively, these objectives enable analysts to capture temporal dynamics of risk and guide interventions that prolong survival or mitigate failure.
