# Question 9: What is the main purpose of the negotiations with customer?

**Status:** ✅ Completed | **Words:** 140 | **Generated:** 2024-12-07T13:10:00Z

---

## Answer

The main purpose of negotiating with a customer is to reach a mutually beneficial agreement that aligns the supplier's capabilities with the customer's needs while maximising the joint value created. Negotiations clarify and document critical parameters—scope, quality, price, delivery schedule, and risk allocation—thereby preventing costly misunderstandings later in the project lifecycle. The process uncovers underlying interests such as long-term partnership potential or service-level flexibility, enabling value-creating trades rather than zero-sum concessions. Effective negotiation also builds relational trust and sets communication norms, which are pivotal for complex, high-uncertainty engagements. From an economic perspective, negotiation reveals each party's reservation point and information set, allowing the surplus to be divided efficiently. A well-structured agreement enhances customer satisfaction, secures supplier profitability, and lays the groundwork for repeat business and collaborative innovation.
