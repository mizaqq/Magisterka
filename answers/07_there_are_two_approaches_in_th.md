# Question 7: There are two approaches in the multidimensional data analysis: stochastic (Statistics) and analytical (Data Mining). Describe the pros and cons of both approaches and the examples of their applications.

## Answer

Multidimensional data analysis follows two complementary paradigms: the stochastic approach rooted in statistical inference and the analytical approach embodied in data-mining algorithms. The stochastic approach models the dataset as a random sample, applying techniques such as principal-components analysis, factor analysis, and multivariate regression to draw population-level conclusions with confidence intervals and hypothesis tests. This paradigm offers rigorous uncertainty quantification and interpretable parameter estimates, yet can struggle with massive, noisy, or highly non-linear data structures. The analytical approach emphasises algorithmic discovery of patterns through k-means clustering, association rules, and gradient-boosted trees, optimising predictive accuracy and scalability rather than formal inference. It excels on big, heterogeneous datasets and captures complex interactions, but risks overfitting and often lacks probabilistic measures of reliability. Hybrid workflows combine strengths by using data-mining to screen variables or detect structure and statistical models to validate findings. Applications span credit-risk scoring, market segmentation, genomic feature selection, and sensor data anomaly detection.
