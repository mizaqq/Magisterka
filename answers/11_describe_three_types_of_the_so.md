# Question 11: Describe three types of the Software Maintenance.

---

## Answer

Software maintenance is commonly divided into three principal categories: corrective, adaptive, and perfective. Corrective maintenance fixes defects discovered after deployment, such as logic errors or security vulnerabilities, ensuring the software meets its original specifications. Adaptive maintenance modifies the system so it continues to operate in changed environments—for example new operating systems, hardware platforms, or regulatory requirements—thus preserving usability over time. Perfective maintenance enhances performance, usability, or other non-functional attributes and may add minor features requested by stakeholders to increase business value. Each type requires impact analysis, regression testing, and updated documentation to avoid introducing new faults while maintaining traceability. Effective maintenance strategies allocate effort proportionally—often 20 % corrective, 25 % adaptive, and 55 % perfective—balancing defect resolution with long-term evolution of the product.
