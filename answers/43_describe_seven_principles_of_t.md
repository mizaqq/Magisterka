# Question 43: Describe seven principles of the PRINCE2 method.

**Status:** ✅ Completed | **Words:** 160 | **Generated:** 2025-07-04T00:27:00Z

---

## Answer

PRINCE2 project management rests on seven universal principles. 1) Continued Business Justification: the project must remain viable against its business case throughout its life. 2) Learn from Experience: teams record and apply lessons from prior and ongoing projects. 3) Defined Roles and Responsibilities: a clear organisational structure assigns accountability to business, user and supplier interests. 4) Manage by Stages: planning, monitoring and control occur in discrete stages to enable go/no-go decisions. 5) Manage by Exception: tolerances for time, cost, scope and risk empower lower levels to act while escalating exceptions. 6) Focus on Products: deliverables and their quality criteria drive planning rather than activities alone. 7) Tailor to Suit the Project Environment: processes, themes and documentation are scaled to project size, complexity and risk. Adhering to these principles ensures PRINCE2 remains adaptable yet rigorous across industries.
