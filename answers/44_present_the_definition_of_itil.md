# Question 44: Present the definition of ITIL. Describe the key elements of service management.

## Answer

ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) is a globally adopted framework of best practices for designing, delivering and continually improving IT services that align with business objectives. In its latest ITIL 4 iteration, service management centres on co-creating value between provider and consumer through four dimensions—organisations & people, information & technology, partners & suppliers, and value streams & processes. Key elements include: 1) The Service Value System, which integrates governance, guiding principles, continual improvement and practices into a holistic model. 2) The Service Value Chain, a set of six activities (plan, improve, engage, design & transition, obtain/build, deliver & support) that transforms demand into value. 3) Management practices such as incident, problem, change-enablement and service-level management that operationalise quality and risk control. 4) Guiding principles—focus on value, start where you are, progress iteratively, collaborate, think holistically, keep it simple, and optimise & automate—that steer decision-making. Together these components foster resilient, customer-centric IT services that adapt to dynamic business needs.
