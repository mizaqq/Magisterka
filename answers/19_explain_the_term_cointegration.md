# Question 19: Explain the term "cointegration of the time series".

**Status:** ✅ Completed | **Words:** 159 | **Generated:** 2024-12-07T13:47:00Z

---

## Answer

Cointegration occurs when two or more non-stationary time series share a common stochastic trend such that a particular linear combination of them is stationary. Although individual series may wander with unit roots, their equilibrium relationship persists, implying long-run economic linkage—for example, spot and futures prices or consumption and income. Testing begins with verifying that each series is integrated of order one, I(1), via unit-root tests. The Engle–Granger two-step procedure then estimates the residuals from a long-run regression and applies an Augmented Dickey–Fuller test on those residuals; rejection of the unit root implies cointegration. The Johansen maximum-likelihood approach generalises this to multivariate systems, determining the cointegration rank and producing normalised vectors that define error-correction terms. In modelling, Vector Error-Correction Models capture short-run dynamics while forcing deviations back toward long-run equilibrium. Recognising cointegration prevents spurious regressions and supports more accurate forecasting and policy analysis.
