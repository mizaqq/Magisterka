# Question 10: What is the purpose of the Software Engineering?

---

## Answer

The purpose of Software Engineering is to deliver reliable, efficient, and user-centred software systems through systematic, disciplined, and measurable processes. By applying engineering principles across the life-cycle—from requirements elicitation to maintenance—it manages complexity and mitigates risks associated with large codebases and evolving stakeholder needs. Core practices such as modular architecture, version control, and rigorous documentation foster team collaboration and facilitate future reuse. Quality assurance techniques including automated testing, code reviews, and continuous integration ensure that each incremental change meets predefined standards of functionality, performance, and security. Structured project management and estimation models help keep development on schedule and within budget, balancing technical scope with economic constraints. Ultimately, software engineering maximises long-term value by producing maintainable, scalable, and secure software that adapts to technological change while satisfying stakeholder objectives.
